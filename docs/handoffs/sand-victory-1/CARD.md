## What exists and what is missing

- `objective.ts` already derives the Hormuz transit objective from its actual end events and gate. `demoConfig.ts` wins when the living Nimitz enters the gate and loses when it is destroyed. Time and score do not end this scenario. Existing objective, terminal and two-plan tests protect those facts.
- `events.ts` already implements `ChangeScore`, `SideScore`, `EndScenario`, event dependencies and final-result priority. `engine.ts` finishes combat before choosing the result; defeat beats draw beats victory. `SimulationSession` freezes the ended run and resets it. Do not replace these systems.
- `TerminalPlay.tsx` already has VICTORY/DEFEAT/DRAW, DECIDED BY, elapsed clock, losses, damage, record access and PLAY AGAIN. **The final debrief omits score**, although the top bar displays it. Its deciding text infers every victory/defeat from the transit pairing, even if another terminal event decided a mixed scenario. `missionLines()` also hardcodes “No time limit” and “score … cannot complete or fail” for any recognized transit pairing. Non-transit endings have no equivalent rule presentation.
- The current combat review left a staged-fire outcome receipt unproven, a hold/result strip clipping own-detail titles, and different track numbers for the same selected versus staged target. These are the bounded carries below.
