# Submit a scenario

You can build a Sandkasten scenario and send it to us. This page tells you how.

## Build it in the editor

Open https://csl-sandkasten.com/editor.

1. Name the scenario and write a short description.
2. Add sides. Give each side a name and pick which one the player controls.
3. Place units. Pick a side, pick a platform, and click the map.
4. Draw reference points. Single points for targets. Areas with three or more corners for zones.
5. Write victory conditions in plain words. For example: protect a unit, reach an area, destroy a unit, or a time limit.
6. Write the briefing. This is what the player reads when the mission starts.
7. Press SAVE. Your browser downloads the scenario as a JSON file.

Press PLAY at any time to test the scenario.

## What makes a good mission

- A clear briefing. Say what the player commands, what they must do, and what loses the game.
- Two or more sides. The player needs an opponent.
- Victory and defeat conditions. Every scenario needs at least one way to win and one way to lose.
- Reference points. Use areas for zones the player must reach or hold.
- A stated region and era. Put them in the name or description.

## How it is reviewed

A human reads every submission before anything is published. A scenario is accepted when it opens in the editor without errors and plays without breaking. We may ask you to fix the briefing or the victory conditions. We will not publish your scenario without credit to you.

## How to submit

Open a new issue on the GitHub repository and choose the scenario submission template. Fill in the fields and attach the JSON file, or paste its contents.
