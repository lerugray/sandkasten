# Facility data, terrain and satellite attribution

`hormuz.json` is generated by `scripts/build-facilities.mjs` (fetch date recorded in `meta.fetchDate`).
It combines two open datasets. Everything is open data; no Google Earth or Google Maps data is used.

| Data | Source | Licence | Credit shown on the map |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airports, heliports, runways | OurAirports, https://ourairports.com/data/ (CSV mirror https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/) | Public Domain | "OurAirports (public domain)" |
| Military areas, naval bases, ports, aerodrome polygons, runway centrelines | OpenStreetMap via Overpass API (overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee, overpass.kumi.systems) | ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright | "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" |

`hormuz.json` is a Produced Work/Derived Database of OSM data. It stays under ODbL 1.0: keep the attribution above and share changes to it under the same licence.

## Data notes

- Names are English where `name:en` exists in OSM, otherwise the OSM `name` (often Persian or Arabic script); the local name is kept in `nameLocal`.
- Runway end positions come from OurAirports where charted. Otherwise they are derived from OSM centrelines (`derivedEnds: true`). Some OSM runways have no designator.
- OSM coverage of military sites is partial. Bandar Abbas Naval Base is not mapped as a naval base in OSM.
